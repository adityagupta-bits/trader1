# Site Structure — Traders @ BPHC

Page-by-page architecture and content outline for the official Traders @ BPHC web platform.

## `/` — Landing Page
- **Hero** — Live pulse status ("THE OFFICIAL QUANT & TRADING CLUB OF BITS HYDERABAD"), high-impact tagline ("Where Quantitative Rigor Meets Market Instinct"), CTAs ("Explore Upcoming Events", "Learn About the Club"), and glowing 3D-styled card with the Traders bull emblem.
- **Market Ticker** — Infinite marquee bar streaming simulated indices (Nifty, Sensex, Nasdaq, Traders AUM, Quant Sharpe ratio).
- **Key Metrics** — Active Members (500+), Virtual Capital Deployed (₹50L+), Competitions & Workshops (15+), Fin/Quant Placements (100%).
- **Live Announcements & Featured Events** — Upcoming flagship challenges (Bull Run Mock Stock, QuantQuest Algo Challenge) with direct modal registration hooks.
- **Pillars of Excellence** — Quantitative Strategy, Derivatives & F&O, Equity Research.

## `/about` — About the Club
- **Mission & Vision** — Bridging engineering STEM talent and quantitative capital markets.
- **What We Do** — Mock Stock trading floors, Quant research lab, derivatives bootcamps, and equity valuation leagues.
- **Club Divisions** — Detailed breakdown of Quantitative Research, Derivatives & Technicals, Equity Research, and Tech & Infrastructure.

## `/events` — Competitions & Workshops
- **Category Filter Tabs** — `All`, `Upcoming`, `Past`.
- **Interactive Event Cards** — Poster headers, date, time, venue, description, and status badges.
- **Interactive Registration Modal** — Built-in form validation, direct registration, and confirmation states.

## `/team` — Executive Board & Leadership
- **Divisional Leadership** — Executive Board, Quantitative Strategy, Technical Analysis & Derivatives, Systems & Software, Operations & Outreach.
- **Alumni & Network** — Direct LinkedIn profiles for mentors and leaders.

## `/faq` — Frequently Asked Questions
- **Interactive Accordion** — Detailed answers regarding prerequisites, algorithmic tools (Python/Pandas/Backtrader), paper trading platforms, and recruitment timelines.

## `/contact` — Contact & Campus Presence
- **Interactive Inquiry Form** — Name, Email, Inquiry Subject (Recruitment, Sponsorship & Collaboration, Research Paper), and Message.
- **Campus Headquarters** — BPHC physical location, email, and social channels (Instagram, LinkedIn).
